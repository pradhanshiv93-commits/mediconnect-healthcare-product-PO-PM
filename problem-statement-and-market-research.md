Market & Problem Research

##Problem Statement

Access to healthcare remains challenging for patients in semi-urban and rural regions.
While telemedicine has improved specialist availability, many patients still struggle with:
-Finding and booking specialist appointments
-Managing long-term prescriptions
-Remembering follow-up visits
-Coordinating care across multiple doctors
-Maintaining treatment adherence for chronic diseases


These issues are particularly severe for patients with diabetes, hypertension, cardiovascular disease, asthma, 
and other chronic conditions that require continuous monitoring rather than one-time consultations.

##Market Overview

The digital health market has evolved rapidly with platforms offering online consultations, 
appointment booking, diagnostic services, and medicine delivery. However, most solutions primarily optimize access to healthcare,
while relatively fewer focus on continuous care coordination after the consultation.
MediConnect aims to bridge this gap by combining teleconsultation with long-term patient engagement and care management.

#Gap Analysis 

Current telehealth platforms focus mainly on booking appointments and online consultations but provide limited support for long-term chronic care. Key gaps include:

-Limited follow-up care: Few automated reminders for follow-up visits.

-Poor medication adherence: Lack of personalized medicine reminders and tracking.
-Fragmented health records: Medical history is often scattered across different providers.
-Minimal caregiver support: Family members have limited access to help manage patient care.
-Rural accessibility issues: Many apps require stable internet and are not optimized for low-bandwidth users.

How MediConnect Addresses These Gaps:
  
  -Automated appointment and medication reminders
  -Centralized digital health records
  -Caregiver access and care coordination
  -Digital Prescriptions 
  -Chronic disease tracking and follow-up management
  -Low-bandwidth, user-friendly design for rural and semi-urban users
